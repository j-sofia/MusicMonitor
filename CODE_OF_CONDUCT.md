# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in MusicMonitor a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus criticism on ideas and behavior, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and learn when mistakes happen.

Harassment, threats, sexualized conduct, discrimination, trolling, deliberate
disruption, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and whenever someone publicly represents
the project. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban participants for conduct they consider
inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Report urgent abuse through GitHub's content-reporting tools. Other conduct
incidents should be reported privately to a maintainer using the contact method
on that maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive incident details in
a public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, and
maintainers will avoid conflicts of interest when reviewing them.
